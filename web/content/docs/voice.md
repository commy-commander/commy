## Voice channels

Click a voice channel to join. Everyone in the channel connects directly to each other, peer to peer, over encrypted QUIC. There is no media server in the middle.

- **Mute and deafen** sit at the bottom left, next to your name.
- **Per person volume** is in the menu when you right click a member.
- **Input, output and the mic test** are in Settings.

## Noise cancellation

The noise cancellation button in the Voice Connected panel (also in Settings, Voice Processing) removes fans, keyboards and background chatter with RNNoise, running entirely on your computer. It is free for everyone.

## Screen and game sharing

Use **Share Screen** in a voice channel to share a screen or a single window. Quality presets trade sharpness for smoothness, and none of them are behind a paywall.

## If someone cannot hear you

1. Check the input device in Settings and use the mic test.
2. Make sure you are not muted.
3. Very strict networks can block direct connections. Commy then falls back to a relay, which adds a little latency but keeps working.
