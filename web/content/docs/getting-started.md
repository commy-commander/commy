Commy is designed to be seamless: install it, pick a name, and you are talking to friends within a minute.

## 1. Install the app

Get the installer for Windows, macOS or Linux from the [download page](/download) and run it.

## 2. Pick a username

On first launch Commy asks for a username. Your identity lives on your device as an iroh keypair, so no account is needed to chat, talk or share files.

An account is optional. It links your devices together, and it lives on whichever account server you choose: commy.chat by default, or [your own](/docs/self-hosting).

## 3. Create or join a server

A Commy server is not a rented machine. It lives on its members' computers and stays reachable while at least one member is online.

- **Create one** with the plus button in the server list, then add channels, roles, rules, emojis and sounds.
- **Join one** by clicking an invite link, or by pasting a link or invite into **Add a Server**.

## 4. Invite people

Open **Invite People** in your server and choose **Copy Invite Link**. See [Invite links](/docs/invites) for how links work.

## 5. Talk, share and play

Join a voice channel to talk over a direct encrypted connection. Noise cancellation is one click in the Voice Connected panel, and screen sharing has no paywall. More in [Voice and noise cancellation](/docs/voice).
