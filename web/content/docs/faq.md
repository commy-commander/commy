## Is Commy really free?

Yes. Voice, screen sharing, file sharing, soundboards, emojis and noise cancellation are free for everyone. Servers run on members' machines, so there is no hosting bill to pass on to you.

## Do I need an account?

No. Pick a username and start chatting. An account is optional and links your devices together.

## What happens when everyone in a server is offline?

The server is unreachable until a member comes back, like a LAN party where everyone went home. History syncs when members reconnect. For 24/7 availability, keep one always on member device such as a spare PC.

## Can other people see my IP address?

The peers you connect to directly can, as in any game lobby or direct call. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## Is my data encrypted?

Yes. Every connection is end to end encrypted QUIC. Relays, when needed, only see ciphertext.

## Which platforms are supported?

Windows, macOS and Linux. Mobile apps are planned.

## What makes Commy different?

It is open source Rust with peer to peer transport: no company stores your messages, calls or files, and no premium tier gates quality.
